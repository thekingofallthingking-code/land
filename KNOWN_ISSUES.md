# Known issues and verification gaps

- The available host is Linux. Windows launch, focus and mouse capture, audible OpenAL playback, actual GPU performance and handling feel remain open. See the Windows checklist in HANDOFF.md.
- The crowded city uses deterministic procedural positions and streamed pedestrians. Hostility memory persists while an NPC remains active. Save files retain player state and parked or stolen vehicles.
- Police pursuit, wanted stars and explosives are Section 3 work. The Section 1 radio, rain and skid effects were skipped.

- Autonomous NPC conversations in groups and bench use were deferred. Benches and crowd ambience are present.
- Some shoulder surfaces show slight overlap in the close aiming camera.
